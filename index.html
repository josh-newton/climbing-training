Fingers

Protocol

  • Sets 6 Sets Per Grip Type
  • Reps 1 x 10s Hang (at 80% Max)
  • Rest 1m Between Sets

Assessment

7 seconds on a 20mm edge adding weight until failure with 3-5m rests in between.

Max Hang = ((Bodyweight + Added Weight) / Bodyweight) * 100.

Reassess every 6 weeks.

Pull Ups

Protocol

  • Sets 6 Sets
  • Reps 4 Reps (Weighted)
  • Rest 2m Between Sets

Assessment

2 Reps weighted pull-up adding weight until failure.

Max Pull = ((Bodyweight + Added Weight) / Bodyweight) * 100.

Reassess every 6 weeks.

Core

Protocol

  • Sets 6 Sets
  • Reps 20s hold
  • Rest 1m Between Sets

Assessment

1 x 20s hold.

Max Core = Progress through front lever hold starting with full crunched, extending gradually to a one legged front lever and eventually a full front lever.

Reassess every 6 weeks.

Endurance

Protocol

  • Sets 1 Set
  • Reps 30m Hang With Feet Planted
  • Rest None

Assessment

7s hangs with 3s rest on a 20mm edge at 50% max finger strength. Repeat until failure.

Endurance = time until failure.

Reassess every 6 weeks.
